## The game's camera (uaRO.exe, decomp /root/BornRok/winEXE/decomp/uaRO_decomp.c)

- CView::Update 0x4fa640: +0x40..+0x48 = the player's place (world +0x3c actor +4/+8/+0xc), easing 0x4faca0 (yaw +0x20
  to +0x38, tilt +0x1c to +0x34, distance +0x24 to +0x3c, at +0x28 to +0x40 by 1/10), quake 0x4fa680, build 0x4faf00.
- Build 0x4faf00 (fastcall ECX = CView, prologue 55 8B EC 83 EC 34): eye +0x7c = at - dist * R(tilt, yaw).row2, kept
  above the ground (0x42a8c0); LookAt 0x409540 (thiscall ECX = view +0x94; eye, at +0x28, up +0x88) -> 4x3 row
  matrix; inverse 0x409860 (thiscall ECX = +0xc4; arg +0x94); 0x40e420 is empty.
- Projection: the renderer *(u32*)0x6EE3D8 (size 0x294); SetSize 0x40d760 (thiscall; w, h as ints): +0 f0, +4 f1,
  +8/+0xc the culling slopes, +0x1c/+0x20 cx/cy floats, +0x24/+0x28 w/h, +0x2c/+0x30 cx/cy ints; FOV constants
  0x69574c (h) / 0x695750 (v) degrees, near 0x695748 (10), far 0x695744 (1500).
- Sprites are perspective already: actor render 0x545b40 projects the anchor (0x412c30 -> x, y, rhw) and scales the
  frame by 0x412d60 = rhw * f0 * k. A wider FOV shrinks them with the world.
